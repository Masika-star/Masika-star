# Additional Research Frameworks

This folder contains developing research concepts that complement my work in agricultural economics, agricultural data analysis and rural development.

These are research frameworks in development, not completed empirical studies. They are kept separate from the featured empirical projects so that the status of each piece of work remains clear.

## 1. Climate-smart agriculture, poultry productivity and household income

**Project folder:** [`climate-smart-poultry/`](climate-smart-poultry/)

A developing research concept examining the relationship between climate-smart agricultural practices, poultry productivity and household income. The framework will be refined before any data collection or empirical claims are made.

## 2. Impact evaluation of agricultural and development interventions

**Project folder:** [`impact-evaluation/`](impact-evaluation/)

A developing framework for assessing the effects of agricultural and development interventions. The research design, outcomes, identification strategy and data requirements will be specified before empirical analysis.
